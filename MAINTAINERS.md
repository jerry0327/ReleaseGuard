ReleaseGuard currently has one primary maintainer. The project does not present a larger team or adoption footprint than exists.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer access is earned through sustained, reviewable contribution rather than a single pull request. Relevant signals include:

- repeated high-quality changes with tests and threat-model reasoning;
- constructive issue triage and reviewer participation;
- responsible handling of security-sensitive information;
- reliable follow-through across release and compatibility work; and
- agreement with the deterministic-enforcement and narrow-evidence design principles.

The primary maintainer may invite a contributor first as a regular reviewer, then as a core maintainer after a documented period of collaboration.

## Maintainer expectations

Maintainers must disclose conflicts of interest, avoid self-approving security-sensitive changes when another qualified reviewer is available, preserve backwards-compatibility commitments, and follow the private vulnerability process.

## Succession

If the primary maintainer cannot continue, the preferred successor is an established contributor with demonstrated release and security-review work. Repository transfer, archival, or read-only status should be communicated publicly rather than leaving the project in an ambiguous state.
